# Character concepts, round 4: the round-2 look, slightly less cute

Justin on round 3: "this new version is even worse. Go back to the old animal/humanoid look, just slightly less cute, chibiish is fine." Round 3 (the 4-heads-tall realistic version) is dropped. These are fast 2D storyboard sketches, not game code.

- **Lineup:** `out/r4-lineup.png`. It shows all 10 round-2 concepts plus Kit, Capling, Bramble and Clover, beside a 1 m fence and a 0.75 m table.
- **Shared base:** `out/r4-shared-base.png`. It shows the body, the 16-bone rig and the part slots.
- **Per-concept sheets:** `out/r4-<name>.png`, each with front and three-quarter views.
- **Source:** `make_round4.py`. It reuses round 2's concepts and parts on a tweaked base. Regenerate with `./render.sh`.

## What changed from round 2 (only the base; every concept gets it for free)

- **Head:** 85% of the round-2 size, about 2.8 heads tall instead of 2.3. Still chibi-ish.
- **Legs:** a little longer, so the stance reads less like a toddler.
- **Eyes:** about 30% smaller, with short friendly brows.
- **Blush:** about half as strong.
- **Muzzles and beaks:** sit slightly lower, so the smaller eyes stay clear.
- **Unchanged:** the 1.35 m body on the 16-bone rig, every part slot, the palette and the painted look.

## Concepts (recommended first, same as round 2)

Mitten (cat), Honey (bear cub), Tuft (owl), Quill (hedgehog), Ripple (otter), Nutkin (acorn), Dusk (moth, with a cape), Wick (lantern), Nimbus (cloud), Pebble (tortoise). Kit (fox), Capling (mushroom), Bramble (chibi human) and Clover (rabbit) from round 1 are redrawn on the same base. Pitches are in `concepts-round2.md`.
