## Goal
- Crafting's maker field (#646, crafting-25) puts a `Maker` PlayerId on inventory, world, basket and stash rows. Hovering a made thing in the bag now says "Made by Mara", with Mara's mark beside the name when she has chosen one, and the name alone when she hasn't. Clay pots that carry their maker in Wear (ceramics-15) still work as before. The row's own Maker comes first.
- The emote wheel's Q hint (ui-48) was OnRequest, and nothing ever requested it. It now shows once, the first time another player stands within 10 m.

Files: `ui/Bag.cs` (the row's maker; tooltip shows the mark only when there is one), `ui/MakerMark.cs` (`MakerInfo.HasMark`; ServerMarkLink.Maker falls back to the player's name), `ui/EmoteWheel.cs` (`SomeoneNear`), and a one-line hook in `Main.cs`.

Not covered: basket and claim stash rows build their stacks in Building's and Claims' files, so their tooltips get a maker once those lanes pass `Maker` into `BagStack` (one argument).
