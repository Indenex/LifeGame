## Goal
Justin: "Can you make a character menu when logging in, and a way to delete/create new characters?"
The first screen after connecting: your characters as cards (a small 3D portrait of the chibi in
their look, the name, race and "last played"), Play / New character / Delete, and a faint card for
each free slot (3 per login by default). New opens today's look picker as is (Justin: "can be used
temporarily"). Delete asks you to type the name: "Their belongings are lost. Their claims will
weather away over time." Survival leads the server side (session_01RedPo6NfcLBPNHQNVhC4qf).
