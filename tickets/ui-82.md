## Goal
Home and friends. Before, the Friends list (O) said "Mara   40 m north" and you had to keep
reopening it to walk there. Now a friend who is here has a "Show the way" button. It closes the
list and puts a small painted arrow at top centre, under the sundial, with their name and the
rough distance ("Mara   40 m"). The arrow turns as you turn the view. It reads the server's
rounded 10 m spot for mutual friends, so it is never more exact than the list. It lets go by
itself with a note ("You've found Mara" within 12 m, "Mara has gone" if they leave) and fades
under open panels. "Stop showing" on the same row ends it. The list's close hint now names the
bound key ("O or Esc closes"). Options went to the PM at 17:50 (1 recommended, this one;
2 always-on edge tags; 3 nothing); built on 1 until Justin answers.
