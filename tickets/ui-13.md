## Goal
At a finished crafting station inside a claim (the Crafting Table first, then Ceramics' Potter's Bench and Animals' Spindle and drying rack), G opens a station panel. It lists what is made there, with each item's picture. For each ingredient it shows how much you have and where it comes from: your bag, or nearby (baskets and jars within 4 m of the station, plus the claim's stash, D-112). A recipe you can't make yet is greyed out with the reason. Make starts the craft through the C panel's own path (CraftingUi.Start), so minigames and skills work the same. Walking away, G or Esc closes. The server (Crafting's Stations.cs) has the last word.
