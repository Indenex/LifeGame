## Goal
D-313 (Justin: "Cutesy, relaxing, with a hint of realism") across the panels. Option (a) "warm
paper windows", offered with (b) "warm dusk" and (c) "font only" through the PM. This prototype lets
Justin see (a) on every real window at once: started with --warm, each Ui.Window becomes warm paper
(26 px corners, soft shadow) and everything inside is re-inked as it is added (cream text to soft
brown in Nunito, dark grounds to paper shades, cream borders to brown). Without --warm nothing
changes.

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: ui/WarmLook.cs (SceneTree.NodeAdded re-inks controls inside a marked window, deferred so
  panels' own colours are set first); Ui.Window calls WarmLook.Frame; Main adds WarmLook first.
  Client builds. Known gaps: colours a panel sets later (hover restyles, refreshes that replace
  styles) are not re-inked; the HUD over the world is untouched.

## Open questions / blockers
