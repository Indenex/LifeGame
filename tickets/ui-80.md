## Goal
Follows ui-79's wear arc. Before, a tool that wore out just stopped working, and the server
explained why only when you tried to use it. Now the HUD note says it once, as it happens:
"Your stone axe is nearly spent" when the best one you carry drops to 8% of its life, and "Your
stone axe is spent. Mend it at a repair table" when it reaches 0. The words match the tooltip and
the server's refusal. Nothing is said during the first 2 s after login, so carrying a spent tool
in doesn't announce it.
