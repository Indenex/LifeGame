## Goal
Justin: "just build a shell right now". Groups exist with no perks yet: form one from the right-click
player menu (Invite to group), a member list, leave any time, group chat "/g". Up to 10 members, no
leader; a member offline for a day drops out; the group ends when one is left. The invite waits 60 s
with a soft "wants you in their group" over the inviter's head; E or the menu (Join their group)
accepts. Server decides everything (D-182); perks are data for later (Groups.Perks).
