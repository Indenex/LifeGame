## Goal
Justin: "make it so right clicking a person opens a menu to invite them to group, trade, etc. just being
near them and hitting E shouldn't do anything". Done means a quick right click on another player
(their body or name tag, within 8 m) opens a small menu beside the cursor: Trade, Whisper, Invite to
town (only once you've signed a roster and they aren't on yours), Inspect (town, offices, maker's
mark) and Invite to group (greyed, "Coming soon"). Greyed items say why on hover; Esc or a click away
closes it. E/G near a player does nothing and no prompt shows over them. A right drag still turns
the camera.

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: `ui/PlayerMenu.cs` holds the menu, the `PlayerFacts` the menu reads (from Player,
  PlayerPos, TownSigner, Town, TownOffice, PlayerMark, MyTrade) and the screen pick
  (`PlayerMenuFacts.At`: a box from feet to name tag, nearest to the camera wins).
  `CameraDrag` only captures the mouse once a press moves 6 px; a press released sooner than 250 ms
  without that is a click and fires `Clicked`, which Main hands to the menu. The `claims.trade` Use
  handler is gone from SocialLayer.Trading.cs (E only still says yes to a trade someone asked you
  for; the PM was offered the menu-only option). Whisper opens chat with "/w " (ChatPanel.Open
  prefill, SocialLayer.OpenChat). Every action goes through the server's existing reducers
  (AskTrade checks reach, InviteToTown roster rights and claim distance). KEYMAP updated.
  Preview: `--screenshot-playermenu` (=inspect, =far). Client builds locally.
