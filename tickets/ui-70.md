## Goal
Justin (via the PM, 2026-10-17): "Inventory slots should highlight the item all the time, and put a cute circle around your active item, it's too hard to see what's there." and "They should only be gray from the last item that was there and you're out."
- Every hotbar and bag slot's item sits on a soft light plate (near-white on paper, a faint cream glow on `--cold`) with a small sticker shadow, in full colour (`ui/SlotLook.cs`).
- The slot in hand gets `ActiveRing`: a soft coral ring (gold on cold) just outside the disc with a gentle glow; it springs in with one small overshoot, or just fades with Reduce motion.
- An empty slot whose item ran out shows a grey, lifted ghost of it, with no plate. Nothing else is ever grey.
- The hotbar no longer dims to 45% after 6 s idle (D-045's quiet HUD), so items are always clear.
Render: `tools/render/scenes/ui-70-slots.txt` (bar, ring, ghost, bag) on main and this branch, warm and `--cold`; `--screenshot-hotbar` now also has a ghost in slot 4.
