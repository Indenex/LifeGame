## Goal
Justin dislikes today's fine noisy relief on everything and wants Valheim-like materials: low-poly shapes with small, simple, hand-painted-feeling textures (wood grain, bark, stone, leaves, thatch, wool), with lighting carrying the beauty, cute and warm. Prototype 2 to 4 looks on one test set (log cabin, pines, oaks, boulders, sheep, player) at golden hour and at night by a fire, send descriptions and renders to the PM, and convert nothing in game until he picks.
