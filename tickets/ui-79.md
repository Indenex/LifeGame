## Goal
Tools wear (D-155), but only the tooltip word ("worn", "nearly spent") said so. A tool that is worn
(25% of its life left or less, Durability.WornBelow) now shows a thin arc along the bottom of its
hotbar and bag slots. The arc's length is the life left: butter while worn, coral once nearly
spent (8% or less). Fresh tools show nothing, so the bar stays calm. The hotbar shows the best stack
you carry of that tool. Option 1 of three sent to the PM for Justin (2: an arc on every tool all
the time, 3: words only); option 1 is built meanwhile.
