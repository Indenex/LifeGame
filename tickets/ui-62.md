## Progress log
- 2026-10-17 13:20: WarmLook now restyles ProgressBar `background`/`fill` boxes (journal skill bars) without rounding their corners. Builds locally.
- 2026-10-17 13:35: Render B's still of #785 (renders/ui-785-chars-14cb4b8-1.png) showed the character badges hanging below every card and Mara's portrait poking past her card. Root cause: `Card` (ui-33) measured its content in `_GetMinimumSize`, but Godot never asks a Button script for that (Button computes its minimum size natively), so no Card ever grew to fit; the polaroids only looked right while the 294 px empty frame set the row height. `Card` now writes its measured size into `CustomMinimumSize` (honoured by every Control); minimum heights stay floors. This also makes build-menu and crafting cards grow instead of clipping, as ui-33 intended. The character badges now wrap inside the card (`card.Wrap()`) so "Played 2 days ago" no longer widens Mara's card. Builds locally.
- Esc menu and Controls/Options are dark vignettes over the world by design (cream text on a dimmed scene); not re-inked. Whether to warm them is a look choice for Justin via the PM, only if Render B's stills show a clash.
