## Goal
Justin (crafting-29): "for a tool to be used, it needs to be on your bar and equipped". Crafting's
server part (branch crafting-equip) adds `EquipSlot(index)` and the `PlayerEquip` table, and refuses
chop, dig-with-a-tool, shear and butcher unless the tool is in the equipped slot. Done means a tool's
hotbar key takes it in hand and again puts it away, the slot in hand wears a gold ring with "(in hand)"
in its tooltip and "Stone axe in hand" floats up, tools fill bar slots by themselves like other items,
and an empty slot's key puts away what you hold. The server's refusal ("Put an axe on your bar and
equip it") shows through the usual reducer-error note.

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: IHotbarLink gains `Equipped` and `Equip(index)` (mock and server: EquipSlot, PlayerEquip
  events). HotbarBar.UseSlot equips tools; UseItem on a tool from the bag equips it if it's on the bar,
  or says to put it there. Slot view: gold 2 px ring, warm fill and glow when in hand. KEYMAP updated.
  Preview `--screenshot-hotbar` (berries, hand axe, stone axe in hand). Client builds locally.

- 2026-10-17: Justin picked (b) "every slot equips": any slot's key takes it in hand (toggle), and
  a click in the world (the strike binding) uses what you hold: eat, drink, put on, or set a made
  thing down (placing). With a tool or weapon the click stays the strike; E still does world
  actions (chop, dig, open, pick up). Herd's strike stands aside while a click would use the held
  item (Main: herd.Blocked || hud.Hotbar.ClickUsesHeld). "Berries in hand    Click to eat" floats up.
