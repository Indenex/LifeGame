## Goal
Justin picked look choices at first join (D-233, option B). A player who joins without a chosen look sees a simple picker: skin tone, hair colour, hair cut (Locks, Cropped, Long, Tied back) and beard (None, Short), with a live preview of their character. Done saves it. The picker can be opened again from Options.

## Contract (agreed with Modeling, who puts it in #361)
- `shared/Looks.cs`: colour lists, cut and beard names, `Looks.Valid(skin, hair, cut, beard)`, `Looks.Default(playerId)`.
- Server: public `player_look` table (PlayerId PK, byte Skin, Hair, Cut, Beard) and `SetLook(skin, hair, cut, beard)`, which validates with Looks.Valid. No row means not chosen yet; others draw Looks.Default.
- Client: `PlayerModel.Build(new HumanFigure.Appearance(skin, hair, cut, beard != 0))` for the preview, `PlayerModel.SetLook(figure, look)` to change it live.
