## Goal
Small keepsakes you shape at the Potter's bench, fire like a pot (campfire or kiln), and set down as decoration. They carry the maker's mark, never need tending (D-148), and cost little: 1 tempered clay each. A per-player cap on set-down figurines (like lamps) keeps the world light. This is a look-and-feel choice (D-154): nothing is built past a rough prototype until Justin picks a look.

## The looks (written, for Justin; one word each)
1. **Animal folk (recommended).** Small chunky sheep, hen, deer, dog: the creatures that live in the world, pinched and rounded, with finger marks left in the clay, about the size of a fist. Reads at a glance on a shelf or the ground and matches the world; Animals and Modeling already have the shapes to borrow.
2. **Little people.** A seated person, a parent with a child, a hunter: simple pinched folk on a slab base. More personal and human, but harder to make charming in a low-poly look.
3. **Charm shapes.** Abstract old-world charms: a round-bellied figure, a spiral beast, a sun disc, a standing stone. Fantasy and ancient in feel, which matches the appetite behind the mark glyphs (fantasy, science, runes).
4. **A mixed set.** About six shapes across animals, folk and charms; you choose which to shape. Most variety, most modelling work.

## Rules once a look is picked
- Process `clay.figurine` (tier 3, shape minigame like the pinch pot), 1 tempered clay, the form is fired like a pot (campfire; small enough). New ClayForm appended after Trough, new items (unfired and fired) in Ceramics' id range 100 to 129, a StructureKind or a placed WorldItem like the lamp, whichever Modeling prefers.
- Carries the maker's mark; slip band does not apply (no rim), a painted colour might later.
- Cap per player: 20 set down (D-075: start low).
