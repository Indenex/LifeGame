## Goal
crafting-20 (Crafting, session_01N9APthjRDfjknUvCweqzzN; Justin chose "both"): crafted real-world things (bricks, pots and the like) go to the bag. You can set one down two ways. (1) Right after a craft finishes, the G prompt reads "Place the coil pot" for a few seconds. (2) A Place key (default P) takes the first placeable thing in your bag, and Tab cycles through them. Both open the same ghost preview: the item's own model, see-through, following the ground about 1.5 m ahead. T and Shift+T turn it, click, Enter or G sets it down, and Esc puts it back in the bag. The ghost looks like Building's placement ghost: pale green when it fits, amber when it can't go there. UX/UI owns the prompt, the key (KEYMAP, Controls F1 row and hint, a wiki line via the Wiki lane) and the ghost look and controls. Crafting owns the server reducer, which items are placeable, and the placement rules.
