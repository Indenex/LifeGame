## Goal
crafting-20 (Crafting, session_01N9APthjRDfjknUvCweqzzN): crafted real-world things (bricks, pots and the like) go to the bag. Justin (D-187): no Place key. "You should just be able to drag and drop it out of your inventory, and the ghost placement can lock onto your cursor to put it where you want." Drag a made thing out of the bag row. Its ghost (the item's own model, see-through, Building's ghost look: pale green, amber where it can't go) follows the cursor on the ground within 3 m. Let go, or click, to set it down. T, Shift+T or the scroll wheel turn it; Esc, or dropping it back on the bag, puts it back. Right after a placeable craft, the Use prompt (E) offers "Place the coil pot" for 10 s and opens the same ghost. UX/UI owns the drag, prompt and ghost. Crafting owns the reducer, which items are placeable, and the rules.
