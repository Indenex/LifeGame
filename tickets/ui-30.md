## Goal
Justin: place fires where you want. Crafting's StartFireAt(processId, score, x, z) is server-checked: within reach, at least 0.8 m away, and a hearth beside the point takes the fire. Client side, per Crafting's recommended flow (their options for Justin go to the PM):
- Starting a fire process (G, the hotbar, the C menu) first shows a ring ghost at the cursor on the ground within reach.
- A click (or E or Enter) picks the spot, then the minigame runs, then StartFireAt sends it.
- Esc lets it go: nothing is used up.
- Amber with "A little further from you" when the spot is closer than 0.8 m.

## Design
- Placing.PickSpot(label, minDistance, picked): a spot mode in my placing ghost (cursor to ground, reach clamp, click/Esc/E/Enter). The ghost is a flat ring (TorusMesh) in the same see-through green/amber.
- CraftingUi.PickSpot is set by Main to Placing.PickSpot. Begin asks for a spot before a fire process (after the Missing check); Send() sends StartFireAt with the spot, otherwise StartCraft as before. Without the hook the server picks, as before.
