## Goal
The Esc menu gets an Options page with four sound sliders: Master, Music, Work and effects, and Ambience. All start at 50% for new players; players who already saved a volume keep it. The Audio lane (session_01LeXCWTFVBQcCNVEDdhPek3) applies the levels to its sounds. UX/UI owns the page and `ui/SoundLevels.cs`, which holds the four levels (0 to 1, saved in user://settings.cfg) and drives Godot's Master bus itself.
