## Goal
D-314, Justin's cutesy pick: windows pop open with a soft bounce, buttons squish when pressed, items
wiggle on hover, all subtle and quick (under 200 ms), with "Reduce motion" in Options. Built into the
shared pieces so no panel needs its own code.

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: ui/Bounce.cs listens for nodes added: windows (Ui.WindowMark, set by Ui.Window and on
  hand-framed windows by WarmLook) pop from 92% with a back-ease and fade in over 180 ms each time
  they show; every BaseButton squishes to 93% on press and springs back on release (120 ms); bag
  slots, hotbar slots and cards wiggle (+4, -3, +1.5 degrees, 200 ms) when the mouse comes over.
  Options has "Reduce motion: on/off" (Settings ui/reduce_motion). Main adds Bounce after WarmLook.
  Client builds.
