## Goal
Render B's #477 stills (renders/ui-477b-c6e82ea-3.png) showed the maker's mark glyph grid filling
only the top-left of the panel, with lots of empty space below. Done means the glyph tiles are big
enough to fill the left side about as tall as the pot and colours on the right, and every colour
shown (presets and square) is one a mark may actually have (D-272, Marks' saturation and value limits).

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: glyph tiles 56 to 100 px, four across (ten glyphs make three rows), with a thicker
  stroke. The group tabs wrap on an HFlowContainer at the grid's width. The pot preview is 200 to
  170 px tall. Preset swatches go through `MarkColour.Clamp` like the shade square, which already
  spans exactly the allowed range. A quiet line under the presets says marks keep to earthy,
  readable colours. Client builds locally.
