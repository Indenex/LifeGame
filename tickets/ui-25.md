## Audit findings
1. **Frames differ.** Alpha 0.82 for most, but 0.72 claim, 0.88 look picker, 0.55 chat. Radius 12 for most, but 16 claim, 18 journal. Title 20 for most, 26 journal. Plan: shared `Ui.Frame()`/`Ui.Title()`; ask Journal and Claims before touching their files.
2. **Centre panels overlap.** Equipment (I), build (B), station (E), trade and crafting (C) can all be open at once. Options sent to Justin: (a, recommended) one centre panel at a time; (b) equipment docks left; (c) leave as is.
3. **Walking.** With I/B/J/K open you still walk; only pause, crafting minigame/search and admin stop it. Options: (a, recommended) keep walking; (b) panels pause walking.
4. **Keys.** K, J and Enter are bound outside Controls.cs, so they can't be rebound: ask Integration. TradeWindow's comment says G, but the key is E.
5. **Esc.** Station and trade check the raw Escape key, not ui_cancel.
6. **Text-only UIs.** Station recipe rows (StationPanel), the claim panel, the journal lists. Next: station crafting reuses CraftGrid (after #489).
7. **Mocks waiting on server parts.** The belt (#443), trade, and "Made by" for non-pots (the Maker column).
