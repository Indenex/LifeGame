## Goal
Render B's warm-window pass on main (renders/ui-609m-pair-*.png at dbde1c8) still showed four cold bits: (a) "Take it off", (b) the selected tab chips ("Tools" in C, "Shelter" in B), (c) the Search box, and (d) the empty Feet slot.

The cause of (a) to (c): ui-51 (#671) put warm Button and LineEdit defaults on the root window's theme. Windows and the HUD live under CanvasLayers, and a theme on the root window doesn't reach through a CanvasLayer, so those controls kept Godot's dark grey. `WarmLook` now also merges those defaults into the engine's default theme, which every control falls back to. Anything that styles itself still wins. For (d), the doll slots are drawn as paper circles directly on the warm look (an ink ring, and a coral ring on the chosen slot), instead of relying on the re-inking pass.
