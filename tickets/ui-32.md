## Goal
Justin: "a sundial item, and when it's built, it adds a little curved time of day UI thing at the top of the screen that tracks the time of day and actually moves along with day/night".
- **The arc:** a small curved arc at top centre. A sun travels it from sunrise (left) to sunset (right), driven by the world clock (Atmosphere.TimeOfDay, which follows /time); at night a moon takes the same path.
- **Behaviour:** dawn and dusk tint the ends. Hover gives the time in words, never numbers.
- **When it shows:** only while the player has a sundial in their claim or town (House building's building-18 flag).
Two looks went to the PM for Justin (D-154): 1 painted sky ribbon (recommended, being built), 2 carved sundial plate.

## Design
- client/scripts/ui/SundialArc.cs (CanvasLayer 2) with ArcView drawing:
  - A 220×46 arc at top centre: a soft wide stroke under a fine line. Colours run dawn-amber, pale gold, dusk-rose by day and blue-grey by night, crossing over about 0.06 of a day around sunrise and sunset.
  - Dots at dawn, noon and dusk.
  - By day, a sun disc with a glow; by night, a crescent drawn as a thick arc stroke.
- Words(t): Deep night, The small hours, Dawn, Early morning, Morning, Late morning, Midday, Early afternoon, Afternoon, Late afternoon, Dusk, Evening, Night.
- It fades in and out with Has() and fades under centre panels.
- SundialArc.Room pushes Hud's status line down while it shows (a small hook in Hud.cs). Main adds it with TimeOfDay = atmosphere.TimeOfDay.
- Preview: --screenshot-sundial[=dawn|morning|noon|dusk|night].
