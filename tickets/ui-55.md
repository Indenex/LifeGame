## Goal
Animation's #705 (anim-18) adds Clap, Shrug and Point to `Emotes.All`. Their wheel petals get their own ink doodles instead of the placeholder star:
- **Clap:** two palms meeting, with butter burst lines.
- **Shrug:** a head between raised shoulders, palms up, and a small coral question mark.
- **Point:** a fist with the first finger out, and a dotted coral line ending in an arrowhead.

They're matched by name, so this merges in either order with #705. The wheel's ring now widens with the number of emotes, so seven petals don't overlap: about 132 px for seven, unchanged at 112 for four.
