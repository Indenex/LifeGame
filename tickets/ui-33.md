# ui-33: Build menu cards that never overlap

## Goal
Justin's urgent bug in the released build ("the build UI is fucked up"): build-menu cards overlapped
sideways, cost rows were wider than the card and ran into the next one, and "Needs a claim" sat in the
wrong place. Done means every card in every build tab, the crafting menu (C) and the station panels is
a fixed width, grows taller to fit, wraps its cost row inside itself, and clips its picture, so no two
cards ever overlap.

## Progress log
- 2026-10-17: cause: a plain Godot Button doesn't measure its children, so the grid sized cards by
  their custom minimum size while the content (cost rows) spilled past them. New `ui/Card.cs`: a Button
  that reports its content's height through `_GetMinimumSize`, keeps a fixed width, clips its contents,
  and offers `Line()` (wrapping text) and `Wrap()` (an HFlowContainer row of icons that wraps).
  `BuildMenu.Tile` and `CraftGrid.Card` (the C menu and every station panel) use it.
- 2026-10-17: build menu panel is four card widths across, always: the tabs wrap on an HFlowContainer
  instead of widening the panel, and the grid sits in a vertical ScrollContainer capped at 60% of the
  screen height. Client builds locally (no Godot in this container for a visual check).
