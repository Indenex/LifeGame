## Goal
Justin: "Pick from a panel and give them a bunch of different symbols colors, fantasy ones, scientific, etc and a color picker kinda thing. Also, if an item is inspected, it tells you who made it." A panel (Options → Your mark) with glyph groups as tabs (Nature, Fantasy, Alchemical, Geometric, ...), a grid of glyphs, a colour picker (earthy presets, a hue strip, a shade square, clamped out of neon and pure black), and the mark stamped on a pot as a live preview. Done saves it through the server. Hovering a made thing in the bag shows "Made by <name>" with their mark. Ceramics (session_01Sc3N1RCsurSszfX5YwGzMU) owns the glyph data, the player_mark table, SetMark and stamping; the maker on item rows involves Crafting and Integration.

## Proposed contract (sent to Ceramics 2026-10-17 05:10)
- shared/Marks.cs: Marks.Groups = MarkGroup { Name, Glyphs }; Glyph { ushort Id (append-only), Name, (float x, float y)[][] Strokes (unit square, 0,0 top left) }; Marks.Find(id); Marks.ClampColor.
- player_mark (PlayerId PK, ushort Glyph, uint Color) public; SetMark(glyph, color) validates.
- Maker (uint PlayerId) on made-item rows (InventoryItem, WorldItem, BasketItem, stash), set when a craft finishes.
