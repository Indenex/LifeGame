## Goal
These are the PM's follow-ups from Render B's stills of the merged #567 build menu (renders/ui-567e-d2ce7e3-*.png):
- (a) Pictures were blank boxes for the first moments after opening. Now they're made 4 s after the world loads (not headless), so they're usually ready before you press B. Until a picture is ready, its card shows a faint pencil house that breathes, and each picture fills in on its card as it finishes, without rebuilding the grid.
- (b) Fence, Gate and Leather drying rack were thin, faint line art. Now a light oval of ground sits under every model, the picture light is brighter (1.1 to 1.35, ambient 0.6 to 0.8), and fences and gates are framed closer.
- (c) The build panel is still dark. Checked: it's framed with `Ui.Window`, which calls `WarmLook.Frame`, so on main (after #609 and #629) it is warm paper, and its cards are re-inked to paper shades. The stills were taken before those merged. No change needed.
