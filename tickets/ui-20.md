## Goal
Justin wants pictures instead of tabbing through every placeable. Default (Justin hasn't picked): B opens a picture grid with category tabs (Shelter, Storage, Crafting, Fences and gates, Fire, ...). Each tile shows a rendered thumbnail of the structure, its name and its material costs, greyed when you can't afford it; clicking a tile starts the existing ghost placement (BuildingSystem). Thumbnails come from the real structure models (StructureView) rendered in an offscreen SubViewport at startup, so new kinds get a picture automatically (D-206). Costs come from StructureShape once House building's building-12 folds them in.
