## Goal
Justin, via the PM (2026-10-17 19:59): "just give it its own parchment window, and make all ui
windows and elements moveable by dragging."
1. The group list sits in its own small parchment window (Ui.Window, the same paper as the
   Friends window) at the left edge, so it reads over bright grass. Hidden when you have no group.
2. Every window (anything framed by Ui.Window or hand-framed with Ui.Frame: friends, crafting,
   journal, trade, claim, signboard, Esc menu, Options...) drags by its top 34 px at any time,
   unless the press lands on a button, field, tab, slot or card. HUD pieces drag while Alt is
   held: needs rings, sundial, hint corner (while a hint shows), group window, the way arrow.
   Everything is kept on screen (6 px margin; a smaller game window pulls things in without
   forgetting where you put them), positions save in user://settings.cfg section "layout", and
   Options has "Reset layout". F1 lists "Drag" (fixed row move_ui); KEYMAP has the row.
