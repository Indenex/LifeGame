## Goal
Players put clothes on through a paper-doll equipment panel (D-074), not automatically. Body and feet slots to start. I opens a centred panel: a faint standing figure with a round slot over the body and one over the feet. Click a slot to see what you wear there, a plain line on what it does (no numbers, D-075), a Take it off button, and the clothes you carry that fit it; click one to put it on. I or Esc closes. The server side is animals-9 (Animals lane, session_01SAcVBUUfPFFk1rdXuiQZRU), which sends the worn-slots contract. Until it lands, the panel runs on mock data, and I does nothing in a normal game.
