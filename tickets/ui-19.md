## Goal
Justin: "assign keybinds to slots on the bottom of the screen. These slots should fill up to 10 slots, and have the item be usable by hitting the assigned keybind." Ten round slots along the bottom (replacing the bag row, which moves into the bag panel, ui-18), on 1 to 9 and 0 by default, rebindable on the Controls page. Each slot shows the item's icon, count and key. A new kind of item takes the first empty slot by itself. Drag a slot onto another to swap, off the bar to clear, or from the bag panel onto a slot. Pressing the key uses the item through the server: eat, drink, wear, or hold a tool (the held tool gets a brighter ring). Placeable items open the placing ghost (ui-16). Slots are kept on the server per player.

## Design
- Server `server/spacetimedb/Hotbar.cs`: `hotbar_slot` (Owner, Index, Item) + `my_hotbar` view; `held_item` (PlayerId, Owner, Item, Quality, public); reducers `SetHotbarSlot(index, item)` (0 clears; an item on another slot swaps), `MoveHotbarSlot(from, to)`, `UseItem(item)` which dispatches on `Items.UseOf(item)` to Eat, DrinkFrom, Wear, or Hold (toggle).
- Content as data (docs/CONTENT.md): `ItemDef.Use` (`UseAction` None, Eat, Drink, Wear, Hold) in shared/Items.cs; garments are Wear through `Clothing.All` (`Items.UseOf`), so no per-item switch.
- Client: `ui/Hotbar.cs` (IHotbarLink, MockHotbarLink, ServerHotbarLink), `ui/HotbarBar.cs` (the bar and HotbarSlotView with Godot drag and drop; data `{hotbar: index}` or `{bag: item}`). Hud hosts HotbarBar in place of InventoryBar; Main attaches the server link on connect and sets Blocked. Controls rows hotbar_1..hotbar_10; Subscriptions add my_hotbar and held_item.
