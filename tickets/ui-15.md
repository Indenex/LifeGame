## Goal
Justin wants a visible cue when a sneak attack lands its bonus hit on an animal (Animals' AnimalHit event with Crit, PR #219). It must use no numbers. A crit shows an amber "!" that pops over the animal with a short eight-ray starburst behind it, rises about 24 px and fades within 0.85 s; a killing crit is 30% bigger. Plain hits get nothing new, so a crit stands out. Animals calls `HitPop.Show(position, crit, killed)` from Herd.cs.
