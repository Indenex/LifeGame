## Design
- `client/scripts/ui/CraftGrid.cs` draws the cards. CraftingUi (Crafting's rules: Missing, Begin, HandsOn, Count) hands its functions in, so the grid only draws.
- Categories come from `CraftGrid.CategoryOf`: the process family (id prefix) and what it makes. Tabs: Tools, Fire and food, Cord and weaving, Clay, Hide and clothing, Other; an unknown category gets its own tab after these.
- Search matches the recipe name, the result's name or an input's name, across all categories.
- Makeable cards come first, then by tier.
- A fire recipe shows a drawn flame (FlameIcon). A Placed recipe with no Makes item shows its first input.
- While the search box has focus, CraftingUi.CapturesInput is true, so walking and hotbar keys pause.
- The panel is centred like the build menu; roasts on the fire still list above the cards.
