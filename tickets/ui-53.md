## Goal
Ceramics' #686 gives a set-down jar or trough its maker's mark (`structure_mark`, client `StructureMarks`). Facing one now shows a quiet second line under the Use prompt, for example "Open the jar" with "made by Mara" beneath. The line uses the same slot as "also: ..." and hints, and only shows when there is no runner-up offer.

Files: `ui/Use.cs` (`Use.MakerAt`), `ui/UseKey.cs` (the second line), and a hook in `Main.cs` that finds the marked structure within 0.75 m of the offer's target and names its maker through `Marks.MakerOf` and `ItemLook.MakerName`.
