## Goal
D-323 (PM default while Justin asleep; revisit): emotes are wave, cheer, a little dance and a bow, and UX adds the wheel. Hold Q and a round warm-paper wheel pops open in the middle of the screen, with four round petals (Wave on top, Cheer right, Dance below, Bow left). Each petal has a small ink doodle and its name. Point the mouse at a petal: it grows a little, gets a coral ring and its doodle bobs, and the middle reads its name. Let go of Q to do it. A quick tap of Q leaves the wheel open so you can click a petal. Esc, Q again or a click off the petals closes it. Reduce motion skips the pop.

The petals are Animation's list (`shared/Emotes.cs`, anim-16 #653), and letting go calls `Reducers.Emote((byte)def.Motion)`. The server checks it (1.5 s cooldown) and everyone sees it through PlayerAct; the wheel doesn't ask again within the cooldown. A new emote in that list gets its own petal, with a star doodle until UX draws one. The PM holds #655 until #653 merges (no "/me" stopgap).

Files: `client/scripts/ui/EmoteWheel.cs` (new), a hook in `Main.cs`, an `emote` row in `ui/Controls.cs`, and a row in `KEYMAP.md`. `--screenshot-emotes` opens the wheel for previews.
