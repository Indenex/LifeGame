## Goal
Under "Home and friends": the first time another player stands within 8 m (PlayerMenu.Reach),
the corner hint says once "Right click a player to trade, whisper, or invite them to your town or
group" (the button follows the turn-the-view binding). Shown once per install, and never if the
player already opened the menu. Until now nothing in the game said that right-clicking a player
does anything.
