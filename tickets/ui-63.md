## Goal
Options > Graphics gets a "UI size" slider: Auto, 80%, 90%, 100%, 115%, 130%, 150%, 175%, 200%. It scales every window, the HUD and all on-screen words together (the root window's ContentScaleFactor), never the 3D world. Auto, the default, keeps the designed size on windows up to 1080 lines tall and grows with taller ones (1440p about 133%, 4K 200%), so big screens are no longer tiny print. Saved in user://settings.cfg (`graphics/ui_scale`) and applied at start-up from the Options page's `_Ready`.
