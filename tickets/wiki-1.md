## Goal
A player wiki in `wiki/` (Markdown, start at `wiki/README.md`) that a new playtester reads before or while playing. It covers: what the game is about and the spirit of playing (survive, learn by doing, build a home, play with others); getting started (first fire, food, water, shelter, the Use key and other keys from `KEYMAP.md`); needs (hunger, thirst, cold, wetness, carrying) described qualitatively; crafting and skills in general terms (things get better as you practise, tools open new options); clay and pottery as the first big goal; building, claims, animals and other players. Tone: warm, short pages, hints not solutions. Rules: no exact numbers, no full recipes or ingredient counts, no step-by-step solutions to minigames or discoveries. Write "being wet makes the cold bite harder; some things dry you off faster than others", not "dry in 2.5 min by a fire". Sources: `DECISIONS.md`, `KEYMAP.md`, tickets and code on `main`. Done when the PM has reviewed it and it is merged.
