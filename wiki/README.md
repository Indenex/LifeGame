# LifeGame Wiki

LifeGame is a multiplayer survival and crafting game. You start with nothing and work up from a first fire to fired pottery, permanent buildings and shared land.

This wiki tells you where to look and what to aim for. It does not give full recipes, exact numbers or minigame solutions; working those out is part of the game.
