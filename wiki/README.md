# The LifeGame Wiki

Welcome. LifeGame is a quiet, beautiful world where you start with nothing and work your way up: a first fire, a warm meal, a roof, a clay pot, a home you share with friends.

This wiki points you in a direction. It will not hand you recipes or solve things for you; finding out is most of the fun. If a page ever tells you *exactly* what to do, we wrote it wrong.
