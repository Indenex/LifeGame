# Needs

You have three needs: food, water and warmth. They drain slowly, so you have time to react. They pause while you are offline.

## Food and water
Both go down over time. Water runs out faster than food. Press X to eat the best food you carry; face water and use it to drink. Cooked food fills you more than raw. Once you can make a container, you can carry water.

## Cold
You lose warmth at night, in rain, and when standing in water. Colder air makes it happen faster, and rain makes it worse unless you are under cover. A burning fire nearby keeps you warm. Shelter also helps, and a good shelter with a fire inside is the warmest place you can be.

## Carrying
You can only carry so much. Over the limit you can still pick things up, but you walk more slowly the more you carry. Some crafted items raise the limit.

## Death
If you die, you drop everything you were carrying. You keep your skills and everything in your journal. You respawn with partial food and water and full warmth.
