# Animals

Sheep live in flocks in open ground.

- **Approaching.** Sheep run from players who move quickly near them. Hold Ctrl to sneak and they stay calm.
- **Wool.** Sheep can be sheared with Use.
- **Meat and hide.** Sheep can be killed with a weapon or cutting tool (strike with left click). The rest of the flock flees. Butchering gives meat and hide. Cook meat at a fire before eating it.
- **Lambs.** Flocks have lambs, which follow their mothers until they are grown.
- **Snow.** In snow, a flock keeps close together and wanders less.
- **Regrowth.** Flocks recover slowly over in-game days and never disappear completely.

Shearing and butchering train husbandry, a survival basic.

## Red deer
Red deer live in herds at the edge of the woods. They notice you from much farther away than sheep and run far into the trees, but a player who keeps still does not alarm them. Butchering a deer gives venison and hide, and a grown deer also gives antler.

## Hunting
Strike with a spear or a blade (left click). A spear reaches a little farther and hits harder. A struck animal runs, so expect a chase. Striking from a sneak, while the animal is calm, can land a critical hit with a visible burst. Stalking is the skill behind this: the more you train it, the more often critical hits land and the harder they hit. Each strike wears your weapon.
