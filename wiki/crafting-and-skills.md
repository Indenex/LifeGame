# Crafting and skills

## Crafting
The crafting panel shows what you can make, grouped in tiers. Early on you work with your hands and what you find; as you make tools, more doors open. A stone edge is a better tool than bare hands, and a better tool opens still more.

Some crafts are small games: striking stone, shaping clay. Pay attention, and be gentle. You can quit with Esc.

## Skills
Things you do often get better. Practise a craft and your results improve, and you work faster. Failures teach too, just a bit more slowly.

- The **survival basics** (fire, foraging, digging, simple tools, shelter, tending animals) are open to everyone.
- **Specialities** are where you shine, and there is only so much room for them. Choose with care, and ask friends who choose differently.
- Titles mark your progress. Reach the top of a craft and the minigames can be skipped, though you can still play them if you want a better result.

## The journal
Press J. It keeps track of what you have found and what you can do.
