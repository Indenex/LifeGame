# Building and claims

## Building
Open the build menu and place a blueprint: a see-through outline of the building. Then bring materials. **Anyone** can add materials to a blueprint, so building together is easy. Start small; a simple shelter is a fine first home. Better buildings need more work and better materials, and keep you warmer and drier.

Inside a finished shelter you can lay a sleeping mat. If you die, you wake up there.

## Doors and storage
Doors can be hung, opened, shut and locked. Baskets hold your things.

## Claims
A claim marker stakes out a patch of land for you and your friends. Inside it, things are safer: your buildings do not weather away, and fighting is off unless the owner allows it. The owner can invite members and tidy up buildings inside the claim. Tend your marker, because an abandoned claim fades.

## Weathering
Things you have made and left out in the open, untouched for a long while, wear away. Visit your things now and then, or claim the land they sit on.
