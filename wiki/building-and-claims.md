# Building and claims

## Building
Open the build menu (B) and place a blueprint, a see-through outline of the building. Then add materials to it with Use; hold the key to add them one after another. Anyone can add materials to any blueprint, so groups can build together. You can have only a few unfinished sites at once.

A lean-to is the simplest shelter. Larger buildings need more and better materials, and give more protection from cold and rain.

Inside a finished shelter you can lay a sleeping mat. After you die, you respawn on it.

Some structures, such as the kiln, are also built from the build menu rather than crafted.

## Doors
Doors can be hung, opened and shut with Use. Anyone can open an unbarred door. The player who hung a door, or any member of the claim it stands in, can bar it (Shift+G); a barred door opens only for them.

## Storage
Storage baskets hold items. Outside a claim, anyone can take from a basket. Inside a claim, only members can.

## Claims
A claim marker, placed from the build menu, claims an area of land. Each player can own one claim. You cannot claim land close to the spawn area.

Inside a claim:
- Buildings and items do not weather away.
- Players cannot fight unless the owner allows it.
- Only members can take from storage.
- The owner can invite members and take down any building; its materials drop on the spot.

The claim border appears as a line on the ground when you get close to it. The marker must be tended (Use) now and then. A claim left untended for too long decays.

## Weathering
Anything players made that sits outside a claim, including buildings, baskets and items on the ground, weathers away if nobody touches it for a long time. Using or interacting with it resets the timer. To keep things safe, visit them or put them inside a claim.
