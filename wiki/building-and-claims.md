# Building and claims

## Building
Open the build menu (B). It is sorted into tabs (shelter, storage, crafting, fire, fences and gates, claims), and each entry shows what it costs; entries you cannot afford yet are greyed out. Pick one and place its blueprint, a see-through outline of the building: rotate it with T, and place it with Enter or a click. Then add materials to it with Use; hold the key to add them one after another. Anyone can add materials to any blueprint, so groups can build together. You can have only a few unfinished sites at once.

A lean-to is the simplest shelter. Clay huts, mud-brick houses and log cabins need more and better materials and give more protection from cold and rain. In a house or cabin, a fire inside warms the whole room.

When you are inside a finished shelter, its roof and the walls between you and the camera fade so you can see yourself. They also fade when you walk behind a building.

Inside a finished shelter you have a right to (you placed it, helped build it, or belong to its claim), you can lay a sleeping mat. After you die, you respawn on it. You can have one mat at a time; laying a new one rolls up the old one. If the building is taken down or weathers away, the mat no longer works as a respawn point.

Some structures, such as the kiln, are also built from the build menu rather than crafted.

## Doors
Doors can be hung, opened and shut with Use. Anyone can open an unbarred door. The player who hung a door, or any member of the claim it stands in, can bar it (Shift+E); a barred door opens only for them.

## Storage
Storage baskets hold items. A storage jar is a larger store made from clay; it is too big for an open fire and must be fired in a kiln. A better-made jar holds more. Outside a claim, anyone can take from a basket. Inside a claim, only members can.

## Claims
A claim marker, placed from the build menu, claims an area of land. Each player can own one claim. You cannot claim land close to the spawn area.

Inside a claim:
- Buildings and items do not weather away.
- Players cannot fight unless the owner allows it.
- Only members can take from storage.
- You can build crafting stations.
- The owner can invite members and take down any building; its materials drop on the spot.

The owner can name the claim in the claim panel (K). The name shows when someone walks in and on the land indicator while you are inside.

The claim border appears as a line on the ground when you get close to it. The marker must be tended (Use) now and then. A claim left untended for too long decays.

## Fences and gates
Fences and gates are in the build menu. On a claim, members can run a fence along the border, and a gate on claimed land opens only for the owner and members. Sheep cannot cross a fence. If a claim lapses, its fences stay as ordinary fences and its gates open to anyone.

## Weathering
Anything players made that sits outside a claim, including buildings, baskets and items on the ground, weathers away if nobody touches it for a long time. Using or interacting with it resets the timer. To keep things safe, visit them or put them inside a claim.
