# Other players

The world is shared by up to a few dozen players at once.

## Chat
Press Enter to chat. Only players near you see your messages, and each line also appears in a bubble above the speaker. Type `/me` followed by an action to describe what your character does (for example `/me waves`). Type `/name YourName` to change your name.

## Working together
- Anyone can add materials to a blueprint.
- A claim owner can invite others as members.
- Other players can learn a process by watching you work or by reading a note you leave.
- Players who choose different specialities can make things for each other.
- Other players can see when you are sneaking.

## Trading
Face another player and press Use to ask them to trade. A "wants to trade" sign appears over the player who asked; they say yes with Use or no with Esc. In the trade window, click things you carry to offer them (Shift+click offers the whole stack) and click your offer to take something back. The swap happens only when both players accept. Any change to either offer clears both accepts. Walking apart or pressing Esc calls the trade off. You can receive more than you can comfortably carry.

## Fighting
Left click strikes a player in reach. A weapon hits harder than bare hands.

- Fighting is allowed in the wild.
- Fighting is blocked near the spawn area, and inside claims unless the owner allows it.
- New players are protected for a short time after they first join.
- Dying to a player works like any other death: you drop what you carry and keep your skills.
- Each hit is announced in chat. A player who kills another has their name shown in red to everyone for a while.
