# Your first hour

You wake in a dry clearing in a forest with empty hands. Around it the land rolls in gentle hills, with streams running down the valleys and ponds in the hollows. A good order to work in:

1. **Gather.** Sticks, stones and plant fibre lie on the ground. Pick up some of each (Space), then open the crafting panel (C) to see what they make.
2. **Make a fire.** A fire gives warmth and light and lets you cook. It burns down over time; feed it fuel to keep it going. When you carry fuel and face a fire, the Use prompt shows roughly how long it has left. A new fire is set on clear, level ground in front of you. A fire that burns out leaves a cold hearth; lighting a new fire beside it rekindles it there. Outside a claim, cold hearths disappear after a while.
3. **Find food.** Berry bushes are the easiest food early on. The game tells you when you are hungry.
4. **Find water.** You can drink from streams and ponds. A stream runs a short walk west of the clearing. Later you can carry water with you.
5. **Plan a shelter.** Nights are cold and rain makes it worse. A lean-to is a reasonable first build; you can replace it with something better later.
6. **Check your journal (J).** It lists what you have found and what you can make.

## The Use key

Most actions in the world go through one key, Use (G by default). A prompt below your character shows what Use will do with the thing you are facing. No prompt means Use does nothing there. See [Keys and controls](controls.md).
