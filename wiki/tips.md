# Tips

- Carry spare fuel. A fire goes out if you stop feeding it.
- Get to shelter before night or rain, and before a winter night above all.
- Streams are shallow enough to wade across. Ponds are deeper; walk around them.
- Lost? Head downhill to find a stream.
- Try picking up everything you see at least once. Many materials are not obvious.
- A Use prompt means there is an action available. Read it before pressing.
- Dry clay fully before firing it.
- Sneak near sheep.
- Keep your important buildings and storage inside a claim, or visit them regularly.
- A cracked pot is still useful material.
