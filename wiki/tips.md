# Small tips

- Keep a few sticks in your pocket for the fire.
- Dusk is a good time to be home.
- Rain is a good time to work indoors.
- If a prompt appears, someone built it for you to press.
- Try picking up *everything* at least once.
- A cracked pot is a lesson.
- Look at the sheep; they look at you too.
- If a page here is out of date, tell someone.
