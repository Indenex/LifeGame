## Red deer
Red deer live in shy herds at the edge of the woods. They notice you from far away and run long before you reach them. They give venison, hide and antler.

- **Spear.** Get close without being seen and strike. Sneaking up first matters.
- **Bow.** A bow lets you hit from a distance. It aims for you within a cone in front of you. Hits are less likely at long range and against a running animal. A first bow can take several arrows and a chase; a better bow and better arrows need fewer.
- **Stalking.** A strike from sneaking can land a critical hit, shown by an amber "!" over the animal. Stalking is a skill: the more you practise, the more often it happens and the harder it hits.

In autumn you may hear stags roar.
