## Stations (extend the list after the Crafting Table)
- **Potter's bench:** shaping pots and jars.
- **Spindle:** spinning wool and weaving cloth.
- **Leather drying rack:** working hides into leather.

Some stations need wood from a felled tree, so you will want an axe early.

## Clay and pottery, "Shape it" (replace once the bench ships)
Shaping is done at a Potter's bench inside a claim. It is a minigame; a good wall gives a better pot.
