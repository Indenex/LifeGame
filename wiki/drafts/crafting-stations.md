## Stations
The crafting panel (C) covers the basics: fire, stone tools, digging, cordage, clay preparation and the first hafted tools. You can make these anywhere.

Everything else is made at a station. Stations are built inside a claim you have rights to, and you work standing next to one.

- **Crafting Table:** general items such as baskets and simple clothing.
- **Potter's bench:** shaping pots and jars.
- **Spindle:** spinning wool and weaving cloth.
- **Leather drying rack:** working hides into leather.

A station uses materials from what you carry, from baskets and jars close by, and from your claim's shared storage. Cooking stays at the fire.

Some stations need wood from a felled tree, so you will want a good axe early.
