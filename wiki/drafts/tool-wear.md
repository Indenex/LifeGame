## Tools
Hand tools and worn clothing wear down with use. A worn-out tool stays in your bag but stops working until it is repaired. Repair it at a repair table; mending costs less than making a new one. Stations and buildings do not wear out.

## Tips (add)
- Repair a tool before it breaks rather than making a new one.
