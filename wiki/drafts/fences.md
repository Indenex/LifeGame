## Claim fences (D-157, D-162, D-163; claims-12)
Goes into: `building-and-claims.md` (Claims)
Claim members can fence the claim's border; fences snap onto the border line. A claim gate opens only for members. If a claim lapses, its fences stay as ordinary fences and its gates open to anyone; they then weather away like other things outside a claim.
