# Keys and controls

The game shows a hint for each key the first time you need it. **Hold F1** at any time to see every key.

## Default keys

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W A S D | Walk |
| Hold right mouse and drag | Turn the view |
| Hold Shift | Run (uses stamina) |
| Hold Ctrl | Sneak (walk slowly) |
| Space | Jump (uses a little stamina) |
| F | Dig |
| E | **Use**: act on what you are facing (the prompt shows what it will do). With nothing to use, pick up what lies at your feet |
| Shift+E | Bar or unbar a door you are allowed to lock |
| C | Crafting panel |
| X | Eat the best food you carry |
| B | Build menu |
| I | Your bag beside what you wear. Drag items onto the hotbar, onto a slot to wear them, or out onto the ground; double-click uses an item |
| 1 to 9, 0 | Use what sits on that hotbar slot: eat, drink, wear, or set a made thing down |
| J | Journal |
| K | Claim panel |
| Enter | Chat |
| Left click or Z | Strike |
| Hold Ctrl when starting a craft in the crafting panel | Play the minigame even when your skill lets you skip it |
| M | Music on or off |
| Esc | Menu (keys, sound and music volume on the Options page, quit) |

Panels and minigames have their own keys, shown on screen while they are open.

## Your look
When you first join, a panel lets you choose your skin tone, hair colour, haircut and beard. You can change it later from Options in the Esc menu (Your look). Everyone sees the look you choose.

## Admin commands
Only a world's admins can use these, typed into chat:

- `/give` opens a list of items to give yourself; `/give <item> <count>` gives one directly.
- `/fly` turns flying on or off. Space rises and Ctrl sinks while flying.
- `/time` sets the time of day, for example `/time dawn` or `/time 06:30`. `/day` and `/night` jump to day or night.
- `/season` sets the season, for example `/season winter`.

## Changing keys

Every key can be rebound on the Controls page of the Esc menu. If you rebind a key, the in-game hints and prompts follow your choice; this page shows only the defaults.
