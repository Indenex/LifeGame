# Clay and pottery

Fired clay is the first big goal. A pot means carrying water, cooking, storing, and the start of real building.

## The road, in outline
- **Find clay.** It lies below the surface; dig for it. Dig deeper and you may find better clay. A tool for digging helps you reach it.
- **Shape it.** You work the clay in your hands. A steady touch makes a better pot.
- **Let it dry.** Wet clay is fragile. It dries more slowly in the rain and faster beside a fire. Patience pays.
- **Fire it.** Fire hardens clay into pottery. A pot fired while still damp will crack, but nothing is wasted: the pieces can help the next attempt.

## Later
A kiln fires more at once and gives better results. Bricks and daub make sturdier buildings. How you get there is for you to find out.
